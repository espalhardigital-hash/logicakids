## Requirements

### Requirement: Meaningful Visual Geometry in Theory
The Phase 5 theory examples (rendered by `theory_examples.py`) SHALL include accurate, geometric visual representations instead of relying on text-only SVGs.

#### Scenario: M2-N3 Complex Figures Theory
- **WHEN** the user views the theory for M2-N3 (Complex Irregular Areas)
- **THEN** the rendered SVGs show colored grid cells highlighting full squares and fractional halves, rather than just text stating the math.

#### Scenario: M4-N1 Scale Theory
- **WHEN** the user views the theory for M4-N1 (Graphic Scale)
- **THEN** the SVG renders an illustrative map segment paired with a visible scale bar.
